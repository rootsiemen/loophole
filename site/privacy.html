Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version

This website collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no contact forms and no comments. The Loophole scanner itself runs fully offline on your own machine and collects nothing at all.

What this website is

This is the static documentation site for Loophole, an open-source security scanner. It exists to explain the tool and link to its source code on GitHub.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process standard technical information — such as IP address, browser user agent and request timestamps — in server logs in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This processing is covered by Vercel's privacy policy. We do not use those logs to build profiles or track individuals.

The Loophole tool

The Loophole command-line tool and GitHub Action:

  • run entirely on your own machine or CI runner — your code never leaves your computer;
  • have no telemetry, no analytics and no network calls;
  • require no account, licence key or registration.

Advertising cookies (future)

This website may in future display advertising provided by Google AdSense. If ads are enabled:

  • third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites;
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to sites on the Internet;
  • you can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings, or via aboutads.info.

Until ads are enabled, this site sets no advertising cookies.

External links

This site links to external services such as GitHub. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Questions about privacy can be raised through the Contact page.
